Compensation for a Personal Injury

Personal injury claimants in Arizona can recover various types of compensation, including:

  • Economic Damages To cover quantifiable losses such as medical expenses, lost wages, and property damage.
  • Non-economic Damages To compensate for subjective, non-monetary losses like pain, suffering, and emotional distress.
  • Punitive Damages In some cases, a court may award you punitive damages if the at-fault party's conduct was particularly egregious, serving as a punishment and deterrent for such behavior.

Also, Arizona follows a comparative negligence rule (A.R.S. § 12-2505), meaning you can still recover damages even if you are partially at fault. However, the amount may be reduced by your percentage of fault.
