The Statute of Limitations for Personal Injury Claims in Arizona

In Arizona, a personal injury claim must be filed within two years from the date of the injury (A.R.S. § 12-542). This timeframe can be shortened or extended based on specific circumstances, such as when the injured party is a minor, or the defendant is a government entity.

It is essential to consult with a knowledgeable attorney to protect your right to bring a lawsuit. Your attorney can also help you initiate an insurance claim to address your immediate needs and negotiate a settlement before a lawsuit needs to be filed.
